# Hometown Carpet Care — Website Design System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Business:** Hometown Carpet Care  
**Market:** Chicago, Illinois  
**Primary Audience:** Adults 35+, homeowners and renters  
**Services:** Carpet Cleaning, Rug Cleaning, Sofa/Upholstery Cleaning  
**Primary Conversion:** Book an Appointment  
**Secondary Conversion:** Request a Quote / Call

---

# 1. Design Philosophy

Hometown Carpet Care should feel like a **reliable local Chicago service business**, not a technology company.

The website must communicate the most important information immediately:

1. What do you clean?
2. Where do you serve?
3. How much does it cost / how can I get a quote?
4. When can you come?
5. How do I book?
6. Why should I trust you?

The design should be:

- Clear
- Friendly
- Professional
- Local
- Trustworthy
- Easy to navigate
- High contrast
- Comfortable for users 35+
- Mobile-friendly
- Fast to understand
- Conversion-focused

Avoid:

- Excessive animations
- Trendy layouts that hide information
- Tiny text
- Low-contrast text
- Excessive gradients
- Complicated navigation
- Long paragraphs
- Autoplay video with sound
- Aggressive popups
- Excessive decorative elements
- Unnecessary steps before booking

## Core Principle

> **Don't make the customer think.**

Every section should answer a question or move the customer closer to booking.

# 9. Accessibility

Target WCAG AA contrast.

Minimum requirements:

- Body text: 17px
- Buttons: 16–18px
- Minimum touch target: 48 × 48px
- Strong focus states
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible form labels
- Never communicate information through color alone
- Error messages must include text
- Icons should not replace important labels
- Avoid extremely thin fonts

Use:

**"Book your cleaning today."**

Instead of:

**"Experience our revolutionary residential surface-care ecosystem."**

**"We clean carpets, rugs, and sofas."**

Instead of:

**"Comprehensive textile restoration solutions."**
