Frequently Asked Questions

Confirm every answer below against the company's actual policies before launch.

How long does carpet cleaning take?
Most homes take about 20 to 30 minutes per room. A typical three-bedroom appointment runs one and a half to two hours, including setup.
How long does the carpet take to dry?
Usually 4 to 8 hours. Airflow speeds it up, so we'll suggest opening windows or running fans before we leave.
Do you clean sofas?
Yes. We clean fabric sofas, sectionals, loveseats, and chairs. We test the fabric in a hidden spot before starting.
Do I need to move my furniture?
No. Clear small items and breakables off the floor, and our technician will move ordinary furniture. We don't move pianos, large electronics, or anything on a fragile base.
Do you remove stains?
Most everyday stains come out or improve a great deal. Some, such as old pet damage, dye stains, or bleach marks, can be permanent. We'll tell you honestly what to expect before we start.
What areas do you serve?
We serve homes across Illinois. Call us at (773) 424-2506 and we'll tell you straight away if you're in range.
How do I book an appointment?
Use the to pick a service, date, and time, or call (773) 424-2506. No account required.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. Tell us the rooms or items over the phone, or send the quote form, and we'll give you a price before we book anything.
